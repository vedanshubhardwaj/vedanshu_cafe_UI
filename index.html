ABOUT US

At Valley Grain & Roast we work with generational farmers, coffee pickers, and heritage millers to bring excellent, low-intervention roasts and wild sourdough straight from the agricultural valley. From our organically farmed coffee cherries all the way down to local stoneground flour and daily hand-shaping, we do as much of the work by hand as we can to ensure sustainability and preservation of the bean’s natural beauty.

We started Valley Grain & Roast simply because we believe there’s something really great about slow fermentation and unhurried mornings, and we want to make it available to everyone. Whether you’re enjoying an early pour over with friends or a quiet hour in the sunlit courtyard with fresh bread, a cup of our roast is the perfect companion! To be savored warm and enjoyed by all.
