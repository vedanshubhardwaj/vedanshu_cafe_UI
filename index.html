ABOUT US

At Refuel Eating Point, we work with generational farmers, artisanal roasters, and local bakers to bring delicious, wholesome food and specialty coffees straight to your table. From carefully sourced ingredients to freshly baked breads and handcrafted sips, we prepare everything with care and warmth to deliver an unforgettable dining experience.

We started Refuel Eating Point with a simple promise: great food, honest prices, and a welcoming place where friends and families love to gather. Whether you’re stopping by for a quick energizing bite or enjoying a slow feast on our veranda, Refuel Eating Point is your perfect dining stop.
