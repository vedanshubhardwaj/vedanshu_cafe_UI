Our Origin & Ethos

"We believe coffee and bread belong together—both require yeast, patience, heat, and time."

Founded on the perimeter of old sunlit vineyards, Valley Grain & Roast was built on a simple conviction: respect the agricultural harvest. Just as natural winemakers preserve the grape’s terroir, our roasting profile never masks the origin of the seed.

Every loaf of sourdough and laminated viennoiserie in our pastry vitrine begins with a wild fermented levain fed continuously since 2018. We partner directly with smallholder coffee farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, paying 3.5× Fair Trade minimums for harvest lots dried slowly on raised African beds.
